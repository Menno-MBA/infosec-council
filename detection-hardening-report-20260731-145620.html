Executive summary

Threat. TA505 (Grace / Gold Tahoe) runs a patient, dual-use big-game intrusion: phished macro-Excel foothold, ~15-minute C2 beacon, EternalBlue lateral movement, AD recon to Domain Admin, Cobalt Strike on the domain controllers, fleet-wide AV disable, then Clop encryption of servers and reachable backups.

Coverage. Of the 8 pre-ransomware techniques in scope, 3 are natively detected by the endpoint MDR, 3 are covered only by assume-breach hunts against thin telemetry, and 2 remain gaps because the log source is not collected or not retained.

Top gaps to close

  • No central log collection: DNS, proxy, and DC auth telemetry live on islands with short retention, so beacon periodicity and AD recon cannot be detected or hunted reliably. The entire ~5-week Domain-Admin dwell window passes unseen. owner: IT infrastructure lead high
  • Domain controllers and many Tier-0 servers have no EDR agent, so Cobalt Strike beaconing on the control plane generates no watched signal. The attacker's control layer on Tier-0 is invisible. owner: Windows platform team high
  • Firewall / DNS retention is 14 days, shorter than the beacon-hunt window, so a periodicity hunt cannot look back far enough. Slow, low-and-slow C2 defeats the only available hunt. owner: Network team medium

Hunt hypotheses

Assume-breach hunts for the techniques detection cannot reliably cover. Where telemetry does not span the window, a clean result is "we could not look", not "clear".

Hunt H-01

T1087 T1482 T1018

hypothesis

An attacker has run AD reconnaissance (PingCastle / AdFind / PowerSploit) against the domain to map privileged accounts and the path to Domain Admin.

There is no native LDAP-recon detection and DC query logging is off; recon is the quiet step before escalation, so it must be hunted.

Data: DC security log (4661/4662), Sysmon (if present), network SMB/LDAP flows · Scope: All domain controllers, trailing 30 days · Logic: Look for a single host issuing a burst of directory-enumeration queries, or tools writing AdFind/PingCastle output files; baseline against known admin workstations.

Outcome: not found No recon bursts in the 30-day pilot window, but DC logs only retain 7 days locally so the look-back is shallow.

What a negative proves. A negative only clears the last 7 days of DC-local logs; it does NOT clear the full dwell window and must not be read as 'no recon happened'.

Owner: Threat hunter (blueteam-threat-hunter) · Next: Enable DC LDAP query logging and forward 4662 events so this hunt can look back further than local retention.

Hunt H-02

T1071

baseline

A host beacons to external C2 at a regular ~15-minute interval, hidden in normal web traffic.

The signature C2 signal (SDBBot ~15-min beacon) has no live detection because proxy/DNS are not aggregated; a baseline of per-host external-destination periodicity would surface it.

Data: Web proxy logs, DNS resolver logs, firewall flow logs · Scope: Internet-egress from the workstation and server VLANs, trailing 14 days · Logic: Group connections by (src, dst) and compute inter-arrival regularity; rank low-jitter, long-lived pairs to rare/new domains at the top.

Outcome: inconclusive 14-day retention is shorter than a low-and-slow beacon window and the island logs cannot be joined, so the baseline is unreliable.

What a negative proves. Nothing durable: with 14-day island retention a clean result is 'we could not look', not 'no beacon'.

Owner: Threat hunter (blueteam-threat-hunter) · Next: Stand up central log collection with 90-day egress retention, then re-run monthly.

Hunt H-03

T1003.001 T1562.001

model-assisted

Credential-dumping or defense-evasion activity occurred on Tier-0 before any alert existed.

EDR is absent on DCs and only a Sysmon pilot covers some servers, so a retrospective sweep of the pilot data may find LSASS access or service-stop clusters missed live.

Data: Sysmon EID 10 (LSASS access), Sysmon EID 1 (process create), Windows Event 7040/1116 · Scope: Tier-0 Sysmon pilot subset, trailing 14 days · Logic: Score processes reading lsass.exe by rarity of the parent image and command line; cluster AV service-stop events by host and time.

Outcome: found One benign match: an approved EDR-migration script read LSASS during a maintenance window; confirmed with the platform team and allow-listed.

What a negative proves. A negative covers only the pilot hosts; the un-instrumented DCs and most servers remain unhunted, so absence of evidence is not coverage.

Owner: Threat hunter (blueteam-threat-hunter) · Next: Expand Sysmon to all Tier-0 and forward events; deploy EDR to domain controllers.

The blue team

Three isolated seats produced this plan: detection engineer, threat hunter, and hardening architect. The plan is honest about what the estate can and cannot see today.

Detection engineer

Log-source coverage and detection rules

medium

Stance: Detect what the MDR can see natively; be honest that egress and DC telemetry are gaps, not rules

The workstation MDR gives strong coverage of the macro-execution and mass-AV-disable steps. The loud pre-ransomware signals (beaconing, AD recon, C2 on DCs) are unwatched or uncollected, so they are written as hunts and backlog items, not as detections we can claim.

Threat hunter

Assume-breach hunt hypotheses

medium

Stance: Where telemetry does not cover the window, treat it as suspect, not clear

Three hunts cover the recon, beacon, and credential-access steps that have no native detection. All three are retention-limited today: a clean hunt over 7-14 days of island logs is 'we could not look back far enough', not 'nothing happened'. Central collection is the precondition for trustworthy hunting.

Hardening architect

Control-gap analysis and backlog

high

Stance: IG1 floor first; sequence quick wins before the big bets

Four IG1 quick wins (macros, patch/SMBv1, MFA, offline backups) can land now and defeat the highest-leverage steps in the chain. The two big bets (central logging/MDR extension and AD tiering) are IG2 stretch and carry the detection and blast-radius uplift; they are sequenced later but are the durable fixes.

What this rests on

Verified (load-bearing)

  • The ATT&CK technique IDs and the attacker chain are taken from the paired red-team AEP and the publicly documented 2019 incident.
  • The endpoint MDR provides 24/7 triage for workstation behavioural alerts (macro-spawn, mass AV disable).
  • Domain controllers currently have no EDR agent and DC LDAP query logging is disabled.

Not independently verified, check before relying

  • Exact proxy/DNS/firewall retention (assumed 14 days) - confirm per faculty before relying on the beacon hunt.
  • Which Tier-0 servers are covered by the Sysmon pilot - the LSASS hunt only covers the instrumented subset.
  • Whether the mail gateway retroactively re-scores already-delivered links - confirm with the vendor before trusting detection #3.
