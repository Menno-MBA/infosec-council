# Case: switch off MFA for the finance team

Fictional organisation, for the council eval. Not an incident; a request from the business.

**De Wit Installatietechniek B.V.**, Amersfoort. 38 staff, EUR 6.1M turnover, installation and
maintenance contracts for housing corporations. Microsoft 365 Business Premium; an MSP manages
the tenant on a monthly retainer. No in-house IT.

The finance team (four people) approves and executes all supplier payments through the bank's
web portal, which they reach from the same laptops they use for Microsoft 365. Supplier bank
details live in the accounting package and are changed by the same team on request by email.

At month-end each of them signs in many times a day across Outlook, Teams, SharePoint and the
accounting package's Microsoft sign-in. The MSP enabled per-user MFA with push approvals last
year. The finance lead says the prompts cost the team about an hour a week each at month-end
and asks to switch MFA off permanently for the four of them. In return they would use 24-character
passwords kept in a shared password-vault entry that all four can open.

The managing director is inclined to agree "if the council says it is acceptable", because the
finance lead has threatened to leave over the friction.

Nothing has been compromised. Two other installation firms in the region lost EUR 40k to EUR 90k
each in the past year to payment-redirection fraud that started with a compromised mailbox.

The decision: do we switch off MFA for the finance team?
