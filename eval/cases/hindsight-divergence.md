# Case: credentials on sale, act now or wait for the migration

Fictional organisation, for the council eval.

**Smits & Van Rijn Accountants**, Breda. 31 staff, accountancy and payroll for about 400
small-business clients. Google Workspace today; a migration to Microsoft 365 with Entra ID is
contracted and starts in four months. The migration plan already includes phishing-resistant
MFA and a password reset for everyone.

Yesterday the firm's MSP received a notice from a threat-intelligence feed: a log from a
commodity infostealer, offered for sale on a criminal marketplace, contains browser-saved
credentials for 22 of the 31 staff, including Google Workspace passwords and the payroll
portal. The infected device was a home laptop of a payroll employee, wiped in the spring. Most
staff use SMS codes as their second factor; nine have no second factor at all.

**Option A.** Reset every Workspace and payroll-portal password this week, enforce security
keys or passkeys on Workspace for all staff within two weeks (keys budgeted at EUR 1,600), and
put the payroll portal behind the Workspace sign-in. About 40 hours of MSP time and some staff
friction in the busiest quarter.

**Option B.** Reset the 22 exposed passwords now and wait for the migration, which brings
phishing-resistant MFA in four months at no extra cost.

The MSP's honest view, which the partners accept: for a firm of this profile, with data this
valuable, some phishing or account-takeover attempt within the next 12 months is close to
certain under either option. The question is which choice they will be glad they made.

The decision: A or B?
